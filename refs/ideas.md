# Ten visual ideas

The person already chose the method, so idea 1 is the one being made. The other nine are on record in case it misses.
Each combines two or more references of different kinds.

## Close to this week (3)

1. **Not you. (made)** R01 + R06 + R12/R13. Look: Apple's dark-to-daylight opener with 1875-style big-head photo
   cut-outs. First shot: an atrium, a small figure in a leather jacket crossing, the premise in type. Why now: the
   Polymarket post is hours old and nobody has made the handoff itself. Risk: the cut-outs read as cheap, not as a choice.
2. **The jacket fitting.** R10 + R07. A tailor's card: the jacket is pinned onto a new, smaller body. First shot: the
   empty jacket on a hanger with a price tag. Why now: same. Risk: no line as strong as "Not you".
3. **Cable sold separately.** R13 + R05. A keynote stage, one product, and every part of it is taken away and priced.
   First shot: the GPU alone. Why now: 684 likes on the reply. Risk: one joke for 30 s.

## Stretches (4)

4. **Exit interview.** R14 + R08. Two seated cut-outs at a cafe table; HR asks one question per cut. Risk: needs a
   third character.
5. **Org chart.** R11 + R06. An org chart drawn in code; one photo slides out, another slides in, the reporting
   lines re-route to "Operations". Risk: reads as a slide, not a film.
6. **Good morning.** R05 + R09. Thirty seconds of the same greeting in different rooms until the badge reads
   Tim Nvidia. Risk: needs the audience to know the greeting.
7. **Unboxing the new CEO.** R07 + R12. A box opens, the cut-out unfolds like the folding GPU. Risk: prop-heavy.

## Wild (3)

8. **Paper trail.** R06 + R01. The whole opener shot as a real paper theatre: hands move the cut-outs on sticks.
   Risk: hands are hard to generate and harder to code.
9. **The jacket's point of view.** R10 + R03. Camera inside the jacket as it changes owner. Risk: nobody's face shows.
10. **Silent film.** R08 + R04. Intertitles instead of voices, 16 fps, piano. Risk: the tag jokes need to be heard.

None of these is centred text on a gradient fading in.
