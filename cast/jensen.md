# Jensen (the outgoing opener)

A photo cut-out of Jensen Huang, a real person. His face is never generated or changed by a model: two licensed photos,
cut out. This file is never pasted into an image or video prompt.

## Design

- Premise: the man who has opened every keynote, sent home in the first ten seconds of this one.
- Job and history: founder and chief executive; the leather jacket is the uniform.
- Wants: his entrance, then his jacket. What stops him: a director who answers every question with fewer words.
- One visible flaw: none added. The cut is honest about being a cut: a jaw that drops in a slot.
- Signature detail, readable at phone size: the black leather jacket, then its absence.
- Silhouette: a large head with swept-back grey hair on a small body with arms held out, palms open.
- Why each garment: the jacket is the thing that changes hands; the black T-shirt under it is what is left.
