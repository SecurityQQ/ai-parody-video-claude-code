# Props

Both are on the same generated sheet as the bodies (`sheets/paper_doll_sheet_v1.png`, prompt in `sheets/prompt.md`).

## The jacket

```passport
An empty black leather biker jacket, arms spread wide as if flying, asymmetric zip, belt at the hem, silver snaps,
drawn as a hand-coloured lithograph with fine ink hatching, off-white cut edge. Unbranded, no logos.
```

```motion
Light for its size: it leaves the body on one frame, turns once and a half on the way, and shrinks as it goes into the
room behind. It lags nothing and lands in two hands with one dull thump. Moves on ones.
```

## The card

```passport
A long graphics card with three round fans, a metal bracket at one end and a row of gold contacts, front view, drawn
as a hand-coloured lithograph, off-white cut edge. Unbranded, no logos, no numbers.
```

```motion
Rigid until the word "folds": then the lower half shuts onto the upper half across four frames with one sharp click (its
back shows), and the shut card drops into his hand. It never opens again.
```

File pieces: `cutouts/prop_jacket.png`, `cutouts/prop_card.png` (split into a top and a bottom half by `tools/build_edit.py`).
