# Tim (the new opener)

A photo cut-out of Tim Cook, a real person. His face is never generated or changed by a model: one licensed photo,
cut out. This file is never pasted into an image or video prompt.

## Design

- Premise: the calmest man in technology gets a new stage and treats it exactly like the old one.
- Job and history: ran operations, then the company; every greeting is the same greeting.
- Wants: everyone to have a good morning, and to sell the cable separately. What stops him: nothing. That is the joke.
- One visible flaw: the jacket is two sizes too big. The sleeves cover his hands.
- Signature detail, readable at phone size: one fixed smile, in every frame he is in.
- Silhouette: a large head with short white hair on a small body whose shoulders droop under a jacket made for someone else.
- Why each garment: the navy quarter-zip and grey trousers are his own; the leather jacket on top is not.

```voice

with a pause before the price. Measured: "Good morning! And welcome to Nvidia." 135 words a minute (target 135);
"This is the new R-T-X. It folds." 123 (target 135, within 15%); "The power cable... is sold separately." 141
(target 150, within 15%); "It's a subscription... now." 116, below its 150 target on purpose: a written pause of
0.58 s before "now". Verbal habit: greets first, sells second. Default emotion: pleased.
```

Checked by eye on stills: the head reads at 480p from the wide shot (about 75 px tall there) and from the medium.
Not checked: the accent of the voice, and whether it suits him. A person has to listen.
