# Brief

The request, and how the agent read it. Written before anything was made, on 2026-11-11.

Spend cap: $15 (set by the agent; the request gave none). Allowed paid models: varg image, speech, music and one
480p video model. Estimate before every paid call. Spent in the end: $2.12 ([review/spend.md](review/spend.md)).

## The request

> make a video where tim cook is replacing jensen huang because of the model incident. take their pictures and make a
> funny video with it; make it 480p please
>
> use ten steps [one guide per step: references, moodboards, sounds, video scripts, characters, Blender/three.js,
> video generation, HyperFrames, AI judge, GitHub]
>
> Mostly just take another funny video and adjust it.

The guide links are left out of this public copy; the matching skills are in the [README](README.md#the-10-steps).
It was given as a standing goal, so every stop for approval in the guides was passed without waiting.

## How the agent read it (inferred, not said)

- "the model incident": a news story about Jensen Huang from the week of 2026-11-08 and the pun on "model" that X
  made of it. On 2026-11-11 Polymarket posted that Tim Cook "is reportedly being floated as a potential replacement"
  (9,036 likes when read).
- "another funny video": Apple's September 2026 keynote opener, where Tim Cook says "No, no, no, no, no. Not me.
  That's your guy." and hands the opening to John Ternus (x.com/aaronp613/status/2097785879041351803, 11,562 likes).
  This film is that opener, flipped: Nvidia's opener, and the director hands it from Jensen Huang to Tim Cook.
- "take their pictures": their real photos, freely licensed, as paper cut-outs (see [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)). No
  photo of either man is fed to an image or video model, and neither voice is cloned.
- 480p: 854x480, 24 fps, 16:9.

## Limits the agent set

- It must read as parody at any frame: cut-out look, a PARODY label in every frame, stock voices.
- The story is about the handoff and the jacket. Nobody else from the news story appears or is named. No claim about
  what happened is made beyond the pun on "model".
